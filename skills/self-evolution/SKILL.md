# Self Evolution

After delivery, record the acceptance score, concrete feedback, and root cause.
Group repeated failures into a small proposal scoped to the employee, project,
or routing rule. Replay representative tasks against the baseline and adopt a
candidate only when its measured score improves. Keep proposals versioned and
reversible; never silently overwrite a Skill, role prompt, route, or project
memory. High-impact changes still require explicit review.
